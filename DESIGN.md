# Ashward design bible

## Audience
Adult. This is for the owner, not for children. Dry colony management. Grim pressure is allowed. No gore prose and no weapon-building detail.

## Fantasy
A walled yard in a dead city. Colonists, stocks, buildings, and a night push. The map has no last district. Inspiration is Infection Free Zone and They Are Billions, played as a log rather than a canvas.

## Pillars
1. Text on a phone or a PC. Buttons wrap. A wide screen gets two columns. No joystick and no map canvas.
2. The yard keeps working with the tab closed. A timestamp catch-up runs on the next open.
3. Jobs, buildings, and the night change the same stocks.
4. Endless city. Scouting only adds districts.
5. One scheduled increment at a time after this depth pass.

## Survival rules
- Stocks: food, scrap, meds, ammo, power.
- Jobs: garden, scrap, clinic, watch, rest. Traits change hunger or risk.
- Buildings spend scrap and raise a cap the job can use.
- Night push tests gate against threat and ammo. Failure injures or marks someone missing.
- No game-over screen. A relief camp can start in the same save.
