# Roadmap

Work **one increment at a time**. Mark the active slice as `IN PROGRESS`. When it ships, move it to CHANGELOG and promote the next `NEXT` item.

## Done
- v0.1.0 Playable meadow prototype (movement, combat, loot, XP, save, mobile controls)

## IN PROGRESS
- none (waiting for first review)

## NEXT (ordered)
1. **Inventory + shrine shop** — open a simple item list from pause; spend Sunseeds on Heart Petals and a stronger swipe.
2. **Quests** — shrine keeper asks Pip to collect 8 Sunseeds / shoo 5 Pebblebugs; reward Sprout Cap visually on Pip.
3. **Biomes** — north grove (darker trees, more Shadowseeds), south shore (sand + water pickups).
4. **Better sprites** — replace circles with 16×16 pixel art sheets drawn in-code or as PNG.
5. **Audio** — mute button, step blips, swipe, pickup chime, looping meadow theme.
6. **Day/night tint** — enemies swap tables at dusk; more Puffmoths at night.
7. **More enemy behaviors** — dash, hop, leave a slowing pollen cloud.
8. **World props** — breakable berry bushes, hidden chests, signposts.
9. **PWA install** — manifest + service worker so kids can “Add to Home Screen”.
10. **Co-op ghost** — local second player with a second virtual stick (optional, later).
